SmartZip Modern

Adds a native Windows 11 archive extraction command for the current user.
Includes SmartZip (MIT), AutoHotkey (GPL-2.0) and 7-Zip (LGPL plus BSD/unRAR terms).
Corresponding upstream source archives and full notices are installed with the product.
No existing WinRAR or 7-Zip installation is changed.
